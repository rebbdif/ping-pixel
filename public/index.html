Table tennis vs 5 rivals

Pick Xenia, Egor, Leonid the octopus, Bolt the robot or Zorp the alien. Rounds go to 11 points, win by 2. Each player serves twice in a row (one serve each at 10:10). Two rounds take the match. Your rival plays harder every round.

MOVE
Mouse, finger drag on the screen, ← → keys or the D-pad
SERVE
Click / tap the screen, Space or A
SMASH
Press A just before the ball reaches your paddle
AIM
Hit with the paddle edge to angle the ball. Too wide and it lands out.
SOUND
B toggles beeps

Rules: the ball must bounce once on your side before you hit it, then land on your rival's side. Net, out or a missed ball gives the point away.
